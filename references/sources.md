# Sources and provenance

Check current journal and publisher policies at review time; confidentiality and AI rules can change. Prefer the target venue's own instructions over these general sources.

## Authoritative internet guidance

- COPE, *Ethical guidelines for peer reviewers*: https://doi.org/10.24318/cope.2019.1.9
  - Grounds expertise and conflict checks, confidentiality, bias awareness, evidence-supported and professional criticism, proportionate requests for further work, editor-only handling of integrity concerns, and accountability.
- Nature Communications Biology, *Brief Guide to Reviewers*: https://www.nature.com/documents/commsbio-referee-brief-guide.pdf
  - Grounds the summary-plus-numbered-comments structure, balanced feedback, evidence for novelty judgments, and the distinction between essential work and impact-enhancing extras.
- Elsevier, *How to conduct a review*: https://www.elsevier.com/reviewer/how-to-review
  - Grounds section-level manuscript assessment, journal-instruction priority, reproducibility and ethics checks, constructive author-facing reports, and the need to follow current confidentiality and generative-AI policy.
- EQUATOR Network, *Peer reviewing research*: https://www.equator-network.org/toolkits/peer-reviewing-research/
- EQUATOR Network, *What is a reporting guideline?*: https://www.equator-network.org/about-us/what-is-a-reporting-guideline/
  - Grounds study-type-specific reporting checks and the distinction between complete reporting and the ability to judge quality.

## User-supplied foundation

- Michael W. Marek, *Performing Peer Review* (2021), `/Users/p3jit/Downloads/PerformingPeerReview.pdf`.
  - Incorporated: checks for abstract and keywords; gap, problem, objectives, questions, and significance; conceptual or theoretical framing; context, collection, analysis, and replicability; explicit question-to-result alignment; discussion against prior work; conclusions, limitations, figures, tables, language clarity, and revision-response completeness.
  - Adapted rather than adopted verbatim: reference quality is assessed by relevance and field-appropriate currency, not a universal “half within five years” threshold. Clear, consistent language is assessed without privileging British or American English or judging author competence.

## Maintenance notes

When updating this skill, preserve the distinction between ethics/policy requirements and optional reviewing technique; reporting completeness and scientific validity; essential claim-supporting revisions and scope-expanding requests; and observations suitable for authors versus sensitive concerns suitable only for editors.
